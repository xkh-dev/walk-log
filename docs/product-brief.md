# Product brief

Walk, Log's boundaries give you a clear test for user-facing changes: the journal should help you remember walks without turning them into a performance task.

## What the app does

Walk, Log lets people record a walk and review their history. A walk can include its date, duration, place, time of day, and companion; optional details stay optional.

People can use the app without an account. Signing in is optional and stores signed-in walks in Supabase.

## The problem

Walks can be meaningful without being exercise goals. General-purpose notes and journals don't always make walk entries easy to find, while fitness products often emphasize targets or performance. Walk, Log provides a focused record without requiring those frames.

## Intended audience

If you walk to think, spend time outdoors, or share time with someone, you can use the app to keep a personal record. It doesn't assume you want to optimize distance, pace, or frequency.

## Product boundaries

- Don't add streaks, scores, badges, or goals.
- Keep walk details optional and allow people to log after the walk.
- Don't add geolocation.
- Preserve the words a person enters.
- Keep the app useful without an account; signing in remains optional.
