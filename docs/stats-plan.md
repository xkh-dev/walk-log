# Stats Plan — Walk, Log

Stats describe the walker back to themselves. They never judge, score, or pressure. No streaks.

## Prioritization
Ranked by S = 0.5·E + 0.35·M + 0.15·P
- E — Easiness: front-end effort using plain JS array methods on local data.
- M — Manager appeal: shows data manipulation and product depth for the portfolio.
- P — PCA alignment: Carl Rogers' Person-Centered Approach — reflect the practice without judgment.

## Phase 1 (MVP dashboard)
- Accumulated Presence — total time + walk count; large editorial type, no rings/gauges. (sum of duration + count)
- Atmosphere & Light — time-of-day distribution as a sunrise→dusk color bar. (counts per time bucket)
- Environmental Landscape — place types as a word column; size/weight scale with frequency, each type its own color, zero-walk types hidden, tiny ones degrade to a color bar + label. (counts per place type)
- Seasonal Rhythm — walks per month as a soft line. (walks grouped by month)

## Additional MVP-friendly ideas
- Distance as meaning — km inferred from duration × average pace, shown as an evocative comparison (e.g. "≈ the Moscow River through the city"), never a goal.
- One-sentence portrait — a warm sentence built from the most common values ("mostly parks, mostly evening, mostly alone").
- Your natural walk — median duration + most common time of day.
- Since you started — days since the first logged walk (honors the practice, not a streak).

## Phase 2+
- Solitude & Connection — two parallel counts; companions shown as warm memory tags, never social stats.
- Duration by Environment — average duration grouped by place (cross-analysis of two variables).
- Spatial Footprint — home city as anchor, other cities as travel milestones.
- Progressive disclosure (Phase 4) — gated reveals once history has real depth.
