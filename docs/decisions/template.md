# ADR template

Record a technical choice by filling in this ADR structure with the context, decision, and consequences future readers need.

## Status

State whether the decision is proposed, accepted, or replaced by another ADR.

## Date

Record the decision date as `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Context

Explain the problem and constraints that make a decision necessary.

## Options

List the realistic alternatives and relevant tradeoffs.

## Decision

State the chosen option and why it fits the constraints.

## Consequences

Record the important benefits, costs, and follow-up constraints.
